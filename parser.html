Protocol Glossary

HL7 Segments (ASTM equiv.)

  • MSH (H): Message Header. Identifies the instrument model and communication date.
  • PID (P): Patient Identification. Holds demographic and patient ID codes.
  • OBR (O): Observation Request. Specifies the lab's requested test and specimen ID.
  • OBX (R): Observation Result. Delivers the quantitative or qualitative machine result with flags.

Technical Flags Explained

  • H / L: High / Low relative to the normal reference range.
  • N: Normal, compliant value.
  • A / AA: Abnormal / Critical Alert. Immediate clinical action required.
